Selected work

Undergraduate dissertation2024–25

Accuracy of 3D human pose estimation from varied viewing angles

This undergraduate dissertation project investigated how the viewpoint of single camera footage affects the accuracy of human motion tracking. The study compared accuracy of 2D and 3D human pose estimation (HPE) systems validated against a Vicon optical motion capture ground truth. To achieve this I developed software to visualize HPE data and compare it with the ground truth in order to calculate error metrics.

Research, European Space Agency INVICTUS project2025–26

Research for Thermal Barrier Coatings

As part of the European Space Agency’s INVICTUS project, I conducted research into thermal protective coatings for the fuselage of a Mach 5-capable aircraft. Reviewed academic literature and liaised with suppliers to assess thermal and mechanical performance of ceramic coating chemistries. Further assessed thermal performance through building a Python-based finite volume model which calculated the thermal lag produced by coatings.
